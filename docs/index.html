Your AI, studied up on every one of your courses.

Super Student copies your Canvas courses onto your Mac and connects them to Claude or ChatGPT. Ask about a lecture, a reading or the midterm, and the answer comes from your actual course materials, with the exact slide, page or minute it came from.

Like a study partner who read everything

Normally your AI only knows what you paste into the chat. Super Student gives it the whole course, kept up to date, so it can answer the way someone who did every reading and watched every lecture would.

Everything from Canvas, on your Mac

Slides, readings, assignments, lecture videos, announcements and grades are copied into a folder on your Mac. Lectures are turned into text right on your computer.

Answers that show their source

Every answer can point to the slide, page or minute it came from. Your AI can also look at the real slide when a chart, diagram or formula matters.

Stays up to date by itself

Every few hours it checks Canvas for anything new. If your professor deletes or replaces a file, your copy is kept in a separate dated folder so old material never gets mixed in.

Getting started will take about 15 minutes

No Terminal and no technical setup. You'll need a Mac, the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app, and your Canvas login.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download Super Student once it's available. Double-click the downloaded file to unzip it if your Mac didn't already, then drag Super Student into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it the first time

    Super Student isn't from the App Store, so your Mac asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

    On macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: open the app, click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the Super Student message. Open the app again.

    On macOS 14 or older: hold Control, click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

  3. Click Set up and follow along

    The app downloads what it needs (about 300 MB, 3 to 10 minutes), then walks you through it:

    • Find your school's Canvas
    • Make a Canvas access key. A button opens the right Canvas page, and the app shows you what to click.
    • Pick your courses
    • Choose Claude, ChatGPT or both

  4. Restart your AI app and ask away

    Quit Claude or ChatGPT completely and open it again so it notices Super Student. Then start a new chat and ask about any of your courses.

Things to ask

Talk to it the way you'd talk to a classmate who never missed a class.

  • What's on the midterm, and what did the professor stress most?
  • Explain slide 14 from Lecture 6 like I've never seen it.
  • Make me a practice exam from weeks 3 to 6, with answers.
  • What did the professor say about the project in the last two lectures?
  • Where did I lose points on my assignments, and what should I fix?
  • What's due this week across all my classes?

Before a big exam, use the study pass from the Super Student window. Your AI reads every document and looks at every picture, saving notes as it goes, so its answers cover the whole course and not only what a search turned up.

Your courses stay yours

It only reads

Super Student never submits, posts or changes anything in Canvas. Like opening a page yourself, reading it can mark it as viewed.

Your access key stays locked up

Your Canvas access key is kept in your Mac's Keychain and is only ever sent to your school's Canvas.

Files stay on your Mac

Your course copy lives in a folder on your Mac. Your AI app reads from it when you ask a question, the same way it would read a file you shared with it.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Super Student is free and open source. You use it with your own Claude or ChatGPT account.

Which AI apps does it work with?

The Claude desktop app and the ChatGPT desktop app on your Mac, plus Claude Code and Codex if you use them. You can connect one or both.

AI in a web browser can't see files on your Mac. For that, click Make exam pack on a course in the Super Student window and upload the pack to any chat.

What's a Canvas access key, and what if I can't make one?

It's what lets Super Student read your courses as you, without your password. You make it once in Canvas (Account, then Settings), and the setup window shows you exactly where.

A few schools turn this off for students. If the button to make one is missing from your Canvas settings, your school is one of them, and Super Student can't connect.

Does it work on Windows, or on an iPad?

Not yet. The app is Mac only, on macOS 13 or newer, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Is there anything it can't copy?

Content that lives outside Canvas itself, like publisher homework sites, some video players and Canvas's newer quiz tool. Each course lists those links so you know what's missing, and you can drop your own notes, textbook PDFs or recordings into the course's My Files folder.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the new version, drag it into Applications to replace the old one, and open it. It updates itself, then reminds you to quit and reopen Claude or ChatGPT.

How do I remove it?

Open Settings in the Super Student window and choose Uninstall. It disconnects your AI apps and removes what it installed. You decide whether to keep your course folder.
